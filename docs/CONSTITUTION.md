## Theme

From the simplest trolley dilemma, an evolving map of moral philosophy grows.  
Not a course that “teaches” morality — a public lab where people *experience* the difference between unconscious collective morality and conscious individuated federation.

**Ambition bar (2028):** Would this still feel groundbreaking? Are we FUCKING BOLD OR OLD?

## Purpose

- Start from absolute minimum (one pure dilemma)
- Evolve through **10 locked turns** (high certainty)
- Beyond Turn 10: **decreasing certainty** by design
- Full **customizability** (value matrix, herd pressure, federation rules)
- Full **LLM / engine transparency** (inspectable traces)

## Ontological axis

| Pole | Engine | Meaning |
|------|--------|--------|
| **Unconscious Collective** | Hive / Herd | Cohesion, majority, “what would they do?”, social cost of deviation |
| **Federation of Individuated Entities** | Personal Matrix | Self-aware value vector; voluntary coordination without mind-fusion |

Neither pole is romanticized. Failure modes of both are first-class content.
